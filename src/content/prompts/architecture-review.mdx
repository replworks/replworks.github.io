## 목적

ARCHITECTURE.md가 PRODUCT_SPEC.md와 TECH_STACK.md를 정확하게 반영하는지 검토한다.

새로운 구조를 제안하거나 문서를 다시 작성하지 않는다.

---

## Prompt

````text
Review these documents together:

- PRODUCT_SPEC.md
- TECH_STACK.md
- ARCHITECTURE.md

Generate only blocking questions.

Check for:

- product requirements without architectural ownership
- components with overlapping responsibilities
- missing inputs or outputs
- undefined data flows
- conflicts with TECH_STACK.md
- architecture decisions that introduce unapproved product behavior
- missing failure boundaries
- contradictory invariants

Do not suggest new features.

Do not choose alternative technologies.

Do not redesign the architecture.

If no blocking questions exist, output:

```text
Architecture can be implemented with high confidence.
```
````
